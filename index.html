The signals that matter

Four numbers that tell you where things stand. Ransomware-linked flaws are the ones criminals use to lock up and extort organizations. Anything new in the last 30 days deserves a fast look. Exploit probability comes from EPSS, a score that estimates how likely a flaw is to be attacked in the next 30 days.

Who and what is affected

The vendors appearing most often, and how much of the catalog is tied to ransomware. A big bar is not a verdict on a vendor. Widely used software simply draws more attention from attackers and researchers.

How likely, how severe

Two different questions. EPSS estimates the odds a flaw gets attacked soon. CVSS rates how much damage it could do if it is. High on both is the worst case. Severity data loads on request because it is fetched one CVE at a time.
